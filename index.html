Projects

aerograph.js

Aerograph — Draw Type With Your Hand

Browser-based hand-tracking app that lets users draw and "type" letters in the air with natural hand gestures.

Real-time gesture recognition with MediaPipe Hands to track finger position and trace strokes.

Interactive canvas capturing traced letters as tiles on a live specimen sheet, with a custom ink-and-seal themed UI.

Custom seal-style controls for confirm, menu and scroll navigation.

signalhand.js — Ongoing

SignalHand — Personalized ISL Translator

Self-trained, two-way Indian Sign Language translator for communication between signers and non-signers.

MediaPipe Hands for real-time hand-landmark tracking in the browser, paired with TensorFlow.js to train and run a custom gesture-recognition model.

Three.js for interactive visual feedback, with live confidence scoring against the trained gesture.

Guided, tab-based workflow: capture signs, train the model, run live translation.
